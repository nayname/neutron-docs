## Leveraged Looping Risks

Leveraged looping in DeFi generally significantly amplifies risks, including:

- Impermanent Loss Exposure
Applying leverage to yield farming can multiply the impact of impermanent loss, especially in volatile market conditions. Losses may far exceed the original stakes.

- Liquidation Events
Leveraged positions are susceptible to liquidations if backing ratios are not maintained, often resulting in significant losses due to rapid market downturns.

- Price Impact and Slippage
High leverage can exaggerate the market impact of large trades, leading to increased slippage and destabilizing price spirals during rapid position unwinding.

- Protocol Instabilities
Leveraged strategies depend heavily on the underlying protocols' stability. Bugs or systemic failures can lead to cascading liquidations across multiple platforms.

- Smart Contract Vulnerabilities
Complex leveraged strategies heighten exposure to smart contract risks, where exploits or failures can lead to substantial financial loss.

## Re-staked Asset De-peg Risk

- In rare cases when a re-staked asset de-pegs from an underlying asset, the vault collateral may become liquidatable since the strategy will be short the native asset perp but collateralized with the restaked asset.

- Liquidity Risks: When the funding rate turns negative, the vault aims to automatically exit short perp and spot positions. However, if there is not enough liquidity in the perp or spot markets exiting a negative funding environment may take longer than optimal.
