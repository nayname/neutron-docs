## Eligible Protocols

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Amber Protocol (AmberFi)">
    #### wBTC Lending on Amber
    **Eligible positions**: wBTC lending positions
    **Reward multiplier**: 3x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

    #### BTC LST Lending on Amber  
    **Eligible positions**: BTC LST lending (eBTC, solvBTC, uniBTC, LBTC)
    **Reward multiplier**: 2x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

    <Info>
    For complete NTRN multiplier details across all activities (1x-6x), see [NTRN Rewards Multipliers](/btc-summer/rewards#ntrn-rewards-multipliers).
    </Info>

    **Access**:
    - **Deposit**: [app.amberfi.io/](https://app.amberfi.io/)
    - **Strategies**: [app.amberfi.io/strategies](https://app.amberfi.io/strategies)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Supervaults">
    #### maxBTC Supervaults
    **Eligible positions**: maxBTC paired with various assets (wBTC, USDC, LSTs)
    **Reward multiplier**: 2x-6x depending on pair (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

    #### BTC LST Supervaults
    **Eligible positions**: BTC LST pairs (eBTC, solvBTC, uniBTC, LBTC with wBTC or maxBTC)
    **Reward multiplier**: 2x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

    **Access**: Through [app.neutron.org/earn/supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/earn/supervaults) or Neutron app interfaces
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Mars Protocol">
    **Planned eligibility**:
    - maxBTC basis trading
    - Cross-collateralized perpetuals positions    
    - BTC derivative collateral strategies

    **Timeline**: Updates will be provided as integration progresses
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Monitoring Your Positions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Campaign Rewards">
    - **Dashboard**: Track points and projected rewards at [app.neutron.org/balance](https://app.neutron.org/balance)

## Phase 1 Specifics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Reward Allocation">
    - **Total Budget**: $250,000 in NTRN rewards
    - **Distribution**: Pro-rata based on points earned
    - **Calculation**: Daily snapshots of deposit values
    - **Boost eligible**: All protocols support NTRN locking boosts
    - **Activity Multipliers**: Different activities earn different NTRN multipliers (1x-6x). See [NTRN Rewards Multipliers](/btc-summer/rewards#ntrn-rewards-multipliers) for complete details
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Important Dates">
    - **Phase Start**: September 17, 2025
    - **Phase End**: November 17, 2025
    - **Duration**: 2 months
    - **Reward Claims**: Available after phase end
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
