**Best for**: Users familiar with Keplr or Leap wallets who want direct protocol interaction, advanced boost mechanisms, and maximum control over their DeFi positions.

To participate directly from Neutron, users don't need to deposit to vaults. Instead, users deposit directly to eligible DeFi positions on [AmberFi](https://app.amberfi.io/) ([docs](https://docs.amberfi.io/)) or [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/earn/supervaults).

<Info>
This guide covers the evergreen process for direct Neutron participation. For current Phase 1 specific protocol eligibility and reward multipliers, see [Phase 1 Details](/btc-summer/neutron-path/phase-1).
</Info>

## Why Choose Direct Participation?

### Advantages
- **Immediate boost access**: No cross-chain coordination needed
- **Direct control**: Manage positions directly through protocol interfaces  
- **No intermediary fees**

### Requirements
- Neutron-compatible wallet (Keplr, Leap)
- Basic understanding of DeFi protocols
- Comfort with direct protocol interaction

<Info>
**New to Neutron?** See our [User Quickstart Guide](/quickstart#for-users) for comprehensive wallet setup and onboarding instructions. 
</Info>

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Set up Your Wallet and Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up Neutron Wallet">
    Set up a Neutron-compatible wallet such as Keplr (recommended) or Leap
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Bitcoin Summer">
    Head to the [Bitcoin Summer Dashboard](https://app.neutron.org/bitcoin-summer), and click **Connect** in the top right
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Tab">
    You will see two options at the top of the page: The **Neutron Positions** tab is for those using the Neutron network, while the **Ethereum Vaults** tab is for those depositing via Ethereum. Choose a position from the **Neutron Positions** tab
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Supervault Deposits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Supervault Position">
    The Protocol describes whether the position is an LP into a Supervault or a lending position in Amber Finance. Choose a Supervault position
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Deposit Type">
    Supervault deposits can be single-sided or in pairs. Input the amount you wish to deposit
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Confirm and sign the wallet extension pop-up. After confirmation, you'll see your position reflected under **My Position TVL**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw (When Needed)">
    To withdraw, click on the **Withdraw** tab, select the percentage you'd like to redeem, and confirm. Withdrawals return both tokens in the vault based on the current pool balance, regardless of whether you entered single- or double-sided
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 3. Lend BTC Assets on Amber Finance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Amber">
    Navigate to [app.amberfi.io](https://app.amberfi.io/) or select Amber positions from the Bitcoin Summer dashboard on Neutron
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and Lend">
    Connect your wallet and lend assets like WBTC, uniBTC, eBTC, or solvBTC to earn lending yield plus campaign rewards
  </Step>
</Steps>


## Boost Your NTRN Rewards (For Neutron Users)

You can boost your NTRN rewards during Bitcoin Summer by up to 3x by staking NTRN for up to 48 months. During your selected staking period, you'll earn ~3% NTRN staking rewards + up to 3x boosted Bitcoin Summer NTRN rewards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Booster Feature">
    To boost your rewards, click the **"Unlock your booster"** button on the Bitcoin Summer Vaults page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Boost Target">
    You can select the current connected wallet by clicking the **"Boost this Neutron Wallet"** button at the bottom or link another address. Once you've successfully linked a wallet, you'll see a "You're boosting" message in the modal
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and Configure Boost">
    At any time, you can monitor your boosts by clicking **"View boost overview"** from the Bitcoin Summer dashboard. You can increase your multiplier by selecting the **"Stake & Lock"** option from the modal
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stake & Lock NTRN">
    You can stake any amount of NTRN for up to 48 months to boost your rewards. Larger deposits and longer stake durations will maximize the size of your multiplier, up to 3x
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maximize Your Multiplier">
    To maximize your boost multiplier, Stake & Lock a dollar value amount of NTRN equal to your deposits in the Bitcoin Summer vaults and increase the lock duration using the slider
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
- If you don't see the option to **"View boost overview"** on the Bitcoin Summer Dashboard, please make sure the Neutron chain is selected and that your Keplr Neutron wallet is connected
- It can take up to 15 minutes after submitting your transaction for the multiplier to appear on the Bitcoin Summer dashboard
</Note>

## How To Withdraw (For Neutron Users)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Your Position">
    To withdraw your balance from the vaults, simply navigate to the position in which you have previously made a deposit
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Withdrawal Mode">
    Toggle the setting in the top right from **Deposit** to **Withdrawal** and enter the amount you'd like to withdraw from your position
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Withdrawal">
    Withdrawals from the vaults must be confirmed using your Neutron wallet
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Phase Timing**: Bitcoin Summer Phases last two months, and you can view the amount of time remaining in a Phase on the Bitcoin Summer dashboard. **Withdrawing your balance from a vault before the end of a Phase will cause you to forfeit your NTRN rewards**.
</Warning>
